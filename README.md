One 1024×1024 front page summing up the world's news of
**Monday, 23 November 2026**: 36 stories across 7 regions
(Europe, Middle East, Africa, Asia-Pacific, North America,
Latin America, and Global markets / science / sport).

`main.py` (Python 3.13, standard library only, no network) copies
`entry.png` to the output directory. Running it twice writes
byte-identical output.

## How the stories were chosen

Researched live on 23 November 2026 (evening CST) from the day's
front pages and wires — OneTurn's 23 November day file (85 stories
from 757 headlines), Reuters, AP, BBC, CNN, The Guardian, France 24,
Al Jazeera, Japan Times, SCMP and eqn.news. Every card below was
verified against at least one live source named here. No invented
facts, no hype, no speculation shown as fact.

Date honesty note: a few of the day's biggest stories reached their
conclusion on Sunday 22 November (COP31, the Dymer strike, the
Merkury sinking, the Delta outage, the ATP Finals final) or Saturday
21 November (BepiColombo's orbit insertion), and were first reported
as the day's news on Monday 23 November by the outlets cited. The
competition counts events that happened *or were first reported* on
23 November 2026 in any time zone; those cards are marked *(reported
23 Nov)* below.
